## Slide 3: Your New Role

### Display

```text
AI = code generator

you = release manager

responsibilities:
  accept / reject changes
  run checks
  deploy
  rollback

why it matters:
  decide what goes online
  know how to undo it
```

### Text

Even if you do not manually write code, you still decide what is accepted, what is merged, what is deployed, and what happens if real users are affected. For solo builders, DevOps is not team bureaucracy. It is the practice of taking responsibility for what goes online.
